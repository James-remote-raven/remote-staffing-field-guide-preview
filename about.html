PURPOSE

Help with the actual decisions.

Remote staffing can involve role design, candidate selection, communication, tools, and support after the hire. This site organizes those topics around questions a team can act on. The guides are written for readers who want to do the work thoughtfully, regardless of how they choose to hire.

EDITORIAL APPROACH

Show the reasoning and sources.

Our articles distinguish practical suggestions from factual claims. When a guide relies on an outside publication, it links the source and explains what that source supports. Examples are illustrative unless identified otherwise. Dates on articles show when the page was last updated.

We expect guidance to change as work practices and evidence change. Readers should check local requirements and their own organizational policies when a decision involves employment terms, privacy, or security.

PUBLISHER

Who publishes this site?

Remote Staffing Field Guide is published by Remote Raven, a remote staffing company. The guides focus on general information readers can use regardless of how they choose to hire.

The guides are informational. They are not a substitute for role-specific professional advice or a company’s own hiring and security policies.
